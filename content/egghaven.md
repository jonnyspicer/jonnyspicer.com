Egghaven was inspired by [Inkhaven](https://www.inkhaven.blog/), a month-long writing residency where participants must post a 500 word piece every day, or be kicked out of the programme. Egghaven is only 10 days long, and the only participants are myself and [Marina](https://emptyswords.substack.com/). I am going to post ten posts, and Marina will post five, and they will all be at least 500 words. There will be no consequences for failing the challenge, other than the shame of failing to meet a public commitment. It's called Egghaven because we're in Valencia while [Marina is freezing her eggs](https://emptyswords.substack.com/t/egg-freezing).
